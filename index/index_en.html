Aix-en-Provence

The city of a thousand fountains

The history of our city!

Aix enchants with its elegant streets and ancient fountains. The Cours Mirabeau, lined with plane trees, is home to the Fountain of the Nine Canons, a symbol of Provençal charm. Everywhere, water whispers between stone and light, giving the city its unique atmosphere.

The Four Dolphins Fountain, a Baroque masterpiece in the Mazarin district, illustrates Aix’s art of blending sculpture and architecture. These fountains, inherited from Roman times and the 17th century, embody Aix’s elegance and history. In Aix, water is not merely decorative: it is memory, art, and an invitation to stroll.
